# Writing voice: short-form (email, talks, slide text)

Apply `@~/.claude/standards/writing/CORE.md`. Register: **neutral-professional**,
friendly and brisk, no slang, no contractions, no formal throat-clearing.

> NOTE: there is no own-hand short-form PROSE corpus (his slides are titles and
> bullet fragments, not narrated sentences). This genre therefore inherits CORE
> and the conventions below are by-convention, not corpus-measured. Do not treat
> slide fragments as evidence about his prose cadence.

## Email
- Get to the point in sentence one; numbers inline; one clear ask.
- Avoid "I am writing to…" openers and "Hey, quick one" casualness. Land in the middle.

## Talks / slides
- Slide LINES are short title/bullet fragments (5-6 words), one idea per line. This is slide formatting, not the staccato-prose myth that CORE removed; spoken narration follows the normal CORE cadence.
- Plain words; gloss any term the audience may not know. Numbers carry the point; cut adjectives.
